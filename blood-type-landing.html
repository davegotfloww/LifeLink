Blood types

Knowing your type can help you reach the right patient faster.

Compatibility is the map that guides each donation. Some blood types are universal, some are rare, and each one matters when the moment is urgent.

Compatibility guide

Every type has a place in the network.

These are the most common matches LifeLink helps coordinate, from universal donors to rare, highly specific donations.

Universal donor

O− can support nearly every patient.

O− is the universal donor because it can be given to all blood types in emergencies when the medical team needs a fast response.

Universal recipient

AB+ can receive from nearly every donor type.

AB+ is the universal recipient, which makes donor matching especially important when supply is tight or a patient needs ongoing care.
