How it works

From a willing vein to a waiting patient, in three steps.

No queues, no cold calls. LifeLink does the searching so a match reaches you the moment it exists.

01

Register

Share your blood type, your location, and the hours you're free to give. Verified once, ready always.

02

Get matched

The moment a nearby patient needs exactly your type, LifeLink alerts you directly — no browsing required.

03

Donate & follow up

Give safely at a partner facility, then receive a note on the life your donation reached.

The Link

Every donation has a name on the other end.

LifeLink was built to close the distance between the two — so giving blood feels like what it is: reaching someone directly.
